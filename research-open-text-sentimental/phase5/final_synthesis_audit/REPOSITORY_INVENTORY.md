## Dataset and experiment chains

### Historical 542 (TPS vs GDS)

- Inputs: `tps_gds_classification/data/raw/tps_response.json` and `gds_response.json`, fetched `2026-03-27` (`phase2/RESEARCH_AUDIT.md`). Labels: `tps_gds_classification/data/updated_labeled_dataset_unique.csv`, `corrected_label` 1 = TPS (86), 0 = GDS (456). Documented in `DATA_CARD.md`.
- Scripts: `tps_gds_classification/scripts/fetch_tps_gds_dataset.py`; trajectory code `trajectory/build.py`; stats `analysis/c1_prime_stats.py`.
- Saved results: `data/c1_prime/c1_prime_metrics.csv`. Recomputed with zero mismatches in `phase5/sentiment_reanalysis/results/historical_542_audit.json`.
- Claim that is saved: author–community divergence 0.371 vs 0.287, Mann–Whitney p = 0.009221. Author means differ; community means do not (Welch p = 0.332). Title-only author tracks: 205 (`historical_542_audit.json` field `title_only_author_fallback_n`).
- Separate and not interchangeable with later S2 labels.

### Undersampled BERT on the same 542

- Saved file: `tps_gds_classification/outputs/bert/metrics.json`.
- Fields: `after_gds_undersample_n` 261 (86 TPS, 175 GDS), `test_size` 40, `test_f1_macro` 0.6354, `test_f1_tps` 0.5625.
- Checkpoints under `outputs/bert/checkpoints/` and `best_model/` account for almost all of the 3 GB. They are training artifacts. Neither current paper uses this test. It is not evidence for the 2,050-post task.
