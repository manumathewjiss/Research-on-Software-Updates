# Figure sources

All five figures are TikZ inside `main.tex`. No external image file is required to compile. The CSV files in `figures/` are local mirrors of selected plotted counts. They are not part of the Overleaf bundle and contain no post identifiers.

These notes are local and unpublished.

## Figure 2, fold-wise lexical terms (`fig:terms`)

Source: `phase5/final_improvement/results/fold_coefficients.json`, generated by `phase5/final_improvement/run_phase_a.py`. For each of the five outer training folds, the word/bigram TF–IDF logistic model was fit without using its held-out fold. The figure prints examples among the 25 largest coefficients in each direction that appeared in all five lists: *error*, *fix*, *tried*, and *issue* toward troubleshooting; *released*, *features*, *improved*, and *updates* toward other update. The figure does not display coefficient magnitudes or claim causal influence. Other high-ranked features include function words and platform vocabulary.

## Figure 5, conceptual framework (`fig:framework`)

This is a schematic, not a quantitative graph. Its four boxes correspond to the paper's separately measured descriptions: purpose detection, sentiment valence, visible diagnostic facts, and first reviewer action. The bottom sentence summarizes the observed non-equivalence. The figure deliberately has no arrows between boxes, because no sequential triage pipeline or causal relation was evaluated.
