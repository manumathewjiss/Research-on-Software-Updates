**Date:** 2026-10-08. **Status:** no manuscript sections written; historical data/results unchanged. This report supersedes optimistic readiness wording in earlier planning notes where it conflicts with saved results. Read the cited files directly before turning any row into manuscript prose. User instructions asked us to assume no deadline for scientific decisions.

## Executive judgment

The project is **ready to start a defensible, carefully bounded empirical paper after a short evidence lock**, but **not yet ready to claim a strong semantic-computing method or a validated developer-triage system**. More bulk labeling and a model zoo are not the answer. The two model-result versions have now been reconciled in `RESULT_VERSION_RECONCILIATION.json`; the authors still need to approve that exact reporting, resolve the relationship to Professor Berhe's 2024 Reddit/update paper, and choose a precise contribution that survives the closest literature. If the goal is a substantially novel ICSC regular paper rather than a careful case study, a new downstream evaluation would add more than another classifier comparison, but that is a new research commitment, not a repair to current scores.

## 2. Corrections to earlier planning records

1. **Reserved and fresh results are separate model fits.** `phase3/experiment_v1/RESULTS.md` states the reserved result was refitted on 2,084 development rows. `phase3/fresh_test_20261007/results_fixed_model/metrics.json` states `development_n=2080` and gives that revised set's SHA-256. Earlier `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` says both were behind one 2,080-post model; that sentence is wrong. The two tests use the same *specification and threshold*, not identical fitted coefficients/training rows. Their score difference is not a controlled estimate of temporal or distribution drift.
2. **“Reddit-only” is version-specific.** The combined 3,362 includes 30 Stack Overflow URL records. The 2,080 development set includes these 30; the Reddit-only sensitivity excludes them. Do not title a dataset table “3,362 Reddit posts.”
3. **The main classifier does not perform end-to-end triage.** It never evaluates `about_software` and `update_related` detection in the fresh 500. Its 0.792 is conditional on a human-established 344-post S2 subset. A workflow diagram must show that boundary.
4. **AI-assisted review provenance is mixed.** `phase3/STATUS_AND_EVALUATION_PLAN.md` and the master status fields record 2,757 assisted decisions; `phase2/annotation/AI_SCREENING_README.md` explains AI proposals are not independent human labels. The corrected fresh/diagnostic sheets are reported as jointly human-written by the user, while earlier similarly valued `AI_Assisted` files were withdrawn. File names/cell identity cannot establish who originated every answer. State only documented workflow, avoid claiming the entire corpus was blindly double annotated.
5. **New close same-lab prior work:** Singh et al. 2024, with Solomon Berhe, already used Reddit feedback for software-update release-note enrichment and impact prioritization. See `FINAL_LITERATURE_AUDIT.md`. Prior planning documents did not include it.
6. **Semantic novelty is weak.** MiniLM did not reliably beat TF-IDF on development folds and was not scored on the fresh test. Off-the-shelf embeddings cannot be sold as a new ICSC semantic method.

## 3. Evidence fit for publication versus unfinished work

**Fit with precise qualifiers:** task rule; collection/review flow; conditional fixed-model results with both class F1s; reserved result explicitly as an earlier fit; fresh result as revised fit; diagnostic-field counts with sampling/conditioning; development robustness as sensitivity; observed 200-post agreement with its provenance/selection caveats; 46-error count and selected examples as exploratory.

**Unfinished/unknown:** exact overlap with Singh et al. 2024's earlier Reddit corpus and API; independently verifiable provenance for all historical labels; blind 92-case qualitative error taxonomy; practitioner validation; automatic update-scope detection; outcome/resolution labels; issue clustering; completely prospective external test; institution's decision about public-post ethics/release of text. None of these may be described as completed.

**Claims to remove:** “first Reddit software-update miner”; “best Naive Bayes overall”; “general discontent” as the entire negative class; sentiment change over time; model reduces developer time; posts are actionable or resolved; diagnostic checklist measures report quality; MiniLM improves the system; 0.863-to-0.792 difference isolates domain shift; 100% agreement validates all 3,362 labels.

## 4. Essential decisions before drafting

1. **Choose the paper's object:** a conditional empirical study of *purpose within reviewed software-update discussions*. If the intended object is an end-to-end Reddit monitor, the current tests do not answer it and additional scope-detection research is essential.
2. **Approve the completed version reconciliation:** `RESULT_VERSION_RECONCILIATION.json` records reserved `2,084 → 159 → 0.863` and revised `2,080 → 344 → 0.792`, with supports and saved file hashes. The authors need to keep the two fits distinct in every future table and describe the later audits as development-only. No retraining is required for this reporting repair.
3. **Confirm the relationship to Singh et al. 2024** with Professor Berhe: conceptual predecessor, shared infrastructure, and any dataset overlap. Cite the paper even if there is no overlap; do not imply the research line began with ICMLA.
4. **Lock the authorship and source-provenance narrative.** Professor Berhe appears on the prior ICMLA manuscript and the 2024 related study, and supplied sources; IEEE authorship must be resolved with the humans who made the intellectual contributions. Do not silently change authors because a chat instruction called Manu “the only author.”
5. **Check source rights and institutional ethics** using `DATA_RIGHTS_AND_ETHICS_CHECK.md` before promising data/model release or locking the final ethics statement. Current Reddit terms raise a real question about permitted model-training and redistribution uses; the project-specific permissions are unknown, not presumed absent.

These decisions can be made alongside manuscript setup. No new code/model run or mass label review is required merely to start a draft.

## 5. Optional research that could materially strengthen a regular paper

The **highest-value new study**, if the authors want a stronger practical claim, is a small, predesigned practitioner/analyst evaluation of whether the distinction or diagnostic fields help identify useful issue reports in a realistic mixed queue. It would need independent relevance criteria, sampling, and outcome measures; it cannot be improvised from the present 80%-positive S2 test. A second option is a separately frozen two-stage scope-plus-purpose evaluation on a genuinely untouched collection. Both are substantive work and may improve contribution, but neither should be added merely to raise reported accuracy. The blind 92-case error coding is lower priority unless the paper makes error taxonomy a main claim.

## 6. Readiness verdict

**Ready to write a bounded empirical paper:** yes, after the claim/version/relationship decisions above are documented; source-rights answers can be resolved in parallel before final methods and release promises. **Ready to submit an excellent ICSC regular paper without further judgment:** no. The principal risk is contribution/venue fit, not lack of more training data. A routine classifier plus descriptive checklist may be judged incremental against the papers in `FINAL_LITERATURE_AUDIT.md`. The PI should first decide whether to accept that narrower empirical contribution or invest in a downstream study. Neither decision should be disguised by a large collection count or a transformer comparison.
