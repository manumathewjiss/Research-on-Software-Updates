# Novelty checkpoint for the ICSC regular paper

**Decision: conditional pass for drafting, not a prediction of acceptance.** The evidence supports an empirical study of a specific software-update discussion task. It does not support claims of a new classifier architecture or the first Reddit software-mining study.

The closest identified predecessor is [Iqbal et al., RE 2021](https://kodu.ut.ee/~kuldarta/Papers/Fail_IKTS2021.pdf): they classify annotated Reddit software posts into bug report, feature request, and irrelevant categories. [Tizard et al., RE 2019](https://kblincoe.github.io/publications/2019_RE_forums.pdf) already study software-forum conversations and information types. [Chaparro et al., FSE 2017](https://personal.utdallas.edu/~vince/papers/fse17.html) study missing information in bug descriptions. A [CHASE 2025 study](https://conf.researchr.org/details/chase-2025/chase-2025-papers/13/Irresponsibility-Killed-the-Cat-Software-Accountability-Concerns) also mines Reddit software concerns at scale. The full comparison and claim boundaries are in `RELATED_WORK_NOVELTY_MATRIX.md`.

Our defensible gap is **conditional update-discussion triage**: after scope review establishes that a post concerns a software update, separate the author's concrete troubleshooting purpose from other update discussion, then characterize which diagnostic details appear in the saved title/body. The study contributes a documented task rule and reviewed corpus, an unchanged fixed model evaluated on a reserved and a fresh sample, a diagnostic-detail profile, and a development-only semantic comparator whose small advantage is uncertain. The fresh-test macro-F1 is lower than the original reserved-test score, which strengthens the need for a bounded generalization claim.

The main scientific risk is that a conventional TF-IDF model and a checklist of diagnostic fields may be viewed as incremental, especially for a regular paper. The checklist does **not** measure developer usefulness, resolution, or information completeness as a validated score. MiniLM did not reliably outperform TF-IDF, so it cannot be sold as the central novelty. A clear problem definition, honest comparison with prior tasks, and transparent error/validity analysis are essential. If the full draft cannot state a concrete insight beyond a new dataset and routine classification, revisit the contribution before submission rather than adding speculative claims.

**Manuscript contribution sentence:** We define and evaluate a conditional triage task for already identified software-update discussions, distinguishing troubleshooting from other discussion; we then describe the diagnostic evidence visible in a reviewed troubleshooting sample and the limits of transfer to a fresh collection.

**Practical use, carefully bounded:** A system could sort already collected update discussions for human inspection. This study has not tested an end-to-end update detector, developer adoption, issue resolution, or Reddit-wide prevalence.

The [ICSC conference scope](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/) includes semantic computing and software-engineering applications; the [submission page](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/submission) provides the current formatting and page-limit instructions. Recheck the 2027 call before submitting. Venue fit and acceptance remain uncertain; these sources provide no paper-specific acceptance probability.
