## Specific contribution to test and write

1. **Task definition:** Distinguish troubleshooting from *other* software-update discussion after software/update scope has been established. Other update discussion includes dissatisfaction, questions, advice, and news; it is not synonymous with discontent.
2. **Audited corpus and evaluation:** Document collection pools, consensus-label provenance, duplicate grouping, a fixed classifier, a reserved test, and an unseen-ID fresh test. The fresh test gives a more conservative result than the reserved test.
3. **Evidence profile:** For a random sample of reviewed Reddit troubleshooting posts, describe which diagnostic details appear in saved title/body. Connect this descriptively to development held-out misses; do not claim that absent fields cause model failure.
4. **Semantic-computing fit:** Compare word features with a semantic embedding model under the same development protocol and analyze meaning-level boundary cases. The frozen MiniLM comparison gives no reliable gain. This is evidence about this task, not a new semantic-computing algorithm.

## Draft positioning sentence

> We study evidence-aware triage of software-update discussions: first identifying whether an already scoped post is troubleshooting, then characterizing the diagnostic details visible in its original text. Our contribution is the task definition, reviewed Reddit corpus, held-out evaluation, and evidence profile—not a new embedding architecture or a measure of issue resolution.
