## Template and paper-writing details

IEEE offers [conference templates in Word and LaTeX](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/authoring-tools-and-templates/). The venue asks for IEEE conference format, so use conference mode, US Letter, and two columns; do not adapt a journal template. The IEEE [paper structure guide](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/structure-your-paper/) recommends a concise descriptive title, a single-paragraph self-contained abstract of up to 250 words, and 3–5 keywords. These are IEEE general writing recommendations; the ICSC regular-paper page does not state a separate abstract word limit. The poster-abstract rule of fewer than 2,000 characters is for posters only.

For this paper, the planned sequence is: introduction and precise research questions; closest related work; collection and human review; task and data splits; fixed model and baselines; reserved/fresh results; diagnostic-detail profile; exploratory error analysis; threats to validity; conclusion. `PAPER_STRUCTURE.md` gives a page budget. Use tables only where they answer a research question. References consume part of the eight pages.

IEEE says methods should be detailed enough to replicate the study and results should be interpreted without exaggeration. [Structure guide](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/structure-your-paper/) In our case, explicitly distinguish the combined 3,362-record collection, the S2 modeling population, the original reserved test, the later fresh test, the development-only sensitivity checks, and the separate 250-post diagnostic sample. State that the classifier acts **after** software/update scope is established. The fixed fresh-test result cannot be replaced by later development refinements. Use `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md` for exact claim-to-result mapping.

## Publication ethics and disclosures to handle before finalizing

- **Authorship:** IEEE defines authors through substantive intellectual contribution, intellectual drafting/revision, and final approval. Professor Berhe's contribution and author status must be resolved honestly with him; this guide makes no unilateral authorship decision. [IEEE ethical requirements](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/ethical-requirements/)
- **Prior ICMLA version:** IEEE permits an evolving research line but requires originality and disclosure of similar prior published/submitted work when relevant. Determine whether any ICMLA version entered proceedings; do not assume acceptance alone means publication. [IEEE submission policies](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-policies/)
- **AI-generated manuscript content:** IEEE says substantive AI-generated text, figures, images, or code included in a submitted article must be disclosed in acknowledgments, identifying the system, affected sections, and level of use. Editing/grammar assistance is generally outside that requirement, though disclosure is recommended. Do not describe AI-proposed labels as human labels. [IEEE submission policies](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-policies/)
- **Public-post ethics and privacy:** IEEE has a policy for research involving human subjects. Whether this public Reddit/Stack Overflow study needs an IRB statement or exemption determination depends on the institution and study details; do not invent approval or exemption. Minimize personally identifying quotations and publish data only under appropriate platform/institutional terms. [IEEE submission policies](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/guidelines-and-policies/submission-policies/)
- **Accurate reporting:** No fabricated citations, shifted test counts, inferred annotator independence beyond the reported workflow, or claim that the 92-case blind error review was done. [IEEE ethical requirements](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/author-ethics/ethical-requirements/)

## Items not confirmed on the public ICSC pages

1. **Anonymous review:** The public submission page does not state that regular papers must be anonymized. It also does not explicitly require author names in the initial PDF. Check the EasyChair track instructions before the final export; do not guess or strip names solely on assumption.
2. **Supplementary materials, code/data link rules, and file-size cap:** not specified on the public submission page. The paper must stand alone within eight pages. Check EasyChair fields before uploading supplements.
3. **Extra-page fee:** the [submission page](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/submission) says up to two extra pages at **$100/page**, but the linked [2027 registration calculator](https://jsp-to-html.replit.app/api/icsc/registration.html) says **$150/page**. The calculator also contains different length language for some nonregular tracks. Avoid extra pages; confirm the price with the organizers if needed.
4. **PDF eXpress:** the venue does not publicly state whether it uses PDF eXpress for initial submission. IEEE says conferences that use it provide a customized invitation and that the [IEEE PDF Checker](https://conferences.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/write-your-paper/meet-ieee-xplore-requirements/) is an alternative. Do not invent a conference ID.
5. **Acceptance odds:** the submission page calls full-paper acceptance historically around 20%; this is a historical venue figure, not an estimate for our manuscript. [Submission](https://www.semanticcomputing.org/submission)
