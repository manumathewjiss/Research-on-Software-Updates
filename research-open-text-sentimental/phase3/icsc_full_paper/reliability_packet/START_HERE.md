# Your 200-post review

1. Read `REVIEWER_GUIDE.md` before entering answers.
2. Reviewer A completes `Reviewer_A_Independent_200_Blank.xlsx`; Reviewer B completes `Reviewer_B_Independent_200_Blank.xlsx`. Decide who is A and B before starting. Each person works **separately** and uses only their own file.
3. In the `Review` sheet, read columns A–D. Fill the yellow columns E–I on **every** row. Fill yellow columns J–Q only when you yourself selected `technical_troubleshooting`; otherwise leave J–Q blank. Use the guide's allowed values and add a short reason in column I.
4. For a long title/body/reply cell, click it and read the full cell text in the formula bar. The visible row height does not show the entire text.
5. Save your completed file under a new name and return it to the analyst. **Do not discuss individual posts or copy answers between files before both completed first-pass files have been saved.** Agreement is measured first; discussion comes afterwards.

These 200 records were selected from audited development data. They exclude the 200 reserved-test posts, 500 fresh-test posts, 250-post diagnostic sample, and 30 non-Reddit development records. The prior labels and collection-pool categories are absent from the reviewer files. The original development and test labels are unchanged.

The two reviews provide a measure of *initial agreement between these two reviewers*. If either person previously saw some posts in earlier project work, disclose that fact; the new pass does not erase that earlier familiarity.
