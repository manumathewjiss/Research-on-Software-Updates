# Qualitative study reviewer guide

**Do not begin until the study coordinator confirms that participation and data-use requirements have been met.** Complete your own workbook independently. Do not discuss any post or view the other reviewer's answers before both first passes are saved.

## Your task

Read **only the supplied saved title and original post body** for each case. Treat the text as the information available for a first engineering triage action. Do not open Reddit, search the web, use comments, or infer that an update actually caused the issue. There may be no single correct action.

If a post mentions several products, updates, or possible issues, focus on **the author's main request** in the title and body. Use the rest as context. If you cannot identify one main request, choose `cannot_assess` and explain the ambiguity. Do not assume that a strongly worded complaint has no technical evidence, or that a technical detail proves an update caused a fault.

Complete every answer column for each row:

1. **First action:** enter `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, or `cannot_assess`.
   - `A` — Open an update-focused investigation lead now. This does not mean an update is proven responsible.
   - `B` — Ask one specific clarification before deciding whether to investigate or monitor.
   - `C` — Monitor or group with similar reports before opening an individual investigation.
   - `D` — No update-focused action is supported by this post.
   - `cannot_assess` — The supplied text does not permit the task; explain why in the final column.
2. **Exact evidence:** quote a short part of the post or identify the precise fact that most influenced you. If none supports an update connection, say that.
3. **Why this action:** explain in one to three sentences why you chose it over the closest alternative.
4. **Important uncertainty:** state what remains unknown; `none identified` is allowed.
5. **One question:** write the one question you would ask the author and how its answer could change your next action; `no question needed` is allowed.
6. **Confidence:** enter `low`, `medium`, or `high`. If low, explain why in the uncertainty or reasoning field.
7. **Cannot assess reason:** fill only when you chose `cannot_assess`; otherwise leave blank.

Judge the **saved snapshot**, even if the author added a fix or update to their post later. A reported fix can affect your first action from the supplied text. The task does not ask you to reconstruct what was visible at publication time or to decide whether a bug truly existed.

For the pilot, note confusing wording in a separate message to the coordinator **after** you submit your independent workbook. The main-study workbook must not be opened or filled until the coordinator releases it after the pilot guide is finalized.
