# AI screening of the 100-post pilot

This is an analytical reading by Codex, prepared at Manu's request on 2026-10-05. It is **not human annotation**, independent inter-annotator agreement, or a replacement for the planned two-person labeling study. Keep the two reviewer sheets blank. Do not use these provisional judgments as gold labels or as a final test set.

The screening uses only the saved text in the blind reviewer sheet and the rules in `REVIEWER_GUIDE.md`. Uncertainty is recorded in the confidence column. The analyst can use the screening to locate ambiguous examples and refine the task definition; any empirical claim about label reliability still requires documented human judgments.

For a faster coauthor check, separate prefilled review files are available as `MANU_VERIFICATION.csv` and `BERHE_VERIFICATION.csv`. Their human decision columns are empty. Follow `COAUTHOR_VERIFICATION.md`; do not describe a proposal as human-reviewed until the corresponding human decision is recorded.
